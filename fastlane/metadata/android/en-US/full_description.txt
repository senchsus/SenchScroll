SenchScroll scrolls your short-video feed for you and writes a readable journal. The app interface is in Russian.

Press "Start", put your phone down, and the app scrolls the feed by itself, opening and reading the comments. For every video an AI writes:
• what the video is about, in its own words;
• what people think in the comments overall;
• a category, a mood and a hype score from 1 to 10.

At the end of the session the AI sums up the whole feed: topics, top-3 videos and the overall vibe.

<b>Features</b>
• Storyboard: up to 9 frames per video are stitched into one image, so each video takes only one AI request.
• Ads and live streams are skipped.
• Human-like timing and swipes.
• Floating pause/stop panel on top of the feed.
• Journal with thumbnails that you can share.

<b>AI providers</b>
Gemini, Groq, Mistral, OpenRouter (free API keys), Grok and Claude (paid API keys). Your key is stored only on your phone.

<b>Notes</b>
• Requires the official TikTok app (TikTok, TikTok Lite and Douyin are supported).
• Uses the Android Accessibility Service API only during a session that you start yourself, to swipe, open comments and read the on-screen text of the video.
• On-screen text, comments and frames are sent only to the AI provider you choose. The journal stays on your phone. Nothing is sent to the developer.
• The app never likes, comments or follows.
